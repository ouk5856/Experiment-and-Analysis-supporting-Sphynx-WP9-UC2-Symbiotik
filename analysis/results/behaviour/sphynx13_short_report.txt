SPHYNX PILOT (N=13) — SHORT REPORT
624 trials (48 each). RT = keypress.rt − image.started. No 18s imputation.
Unanswered = no RT; count as incorrect for accuracy.

────────────────────────────────────────
WHY p_adj IS SOMETIMES NaN
────────────────────────────────────────
Bonferroni adjustment is applied ONLY within pairwise comparison families
(e.g. all non/semi/full Fisher or Mann–Whitney pairs for one outcome × scope).

p_adj stays NaN for tests that are NOT in those families:
  • omnibus tests (Kruskal / chi² / 2-level Fisher omnibus)
  • paired tests (McNemar, Wilcoxon signed-rank)

That is intentional, not a bug. Significance still uses p_raw when p_adj is NaN
(significant if fillna(p_raw) < .05).

────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT WE OBSERVE (POOLED)
────────────────────────────────────────

1) QUESTION COMPLEXITY (strongest effect)
   Q2 is harder than Q1 on every outcome:
   • RT median ~10.7s vs ~6.3s (p ≈ 0)
   • Response rate 87% vs 99%
   • Accuracy 61% vs 92%

2) GRAPH SIZE
   12-node harder than 6-node:
   • RT median ~9.5s vs ~7.3s
   • Response rate 89% vs 97%
   • Accuracy 68% vs 85%

3) ADAPTATION (main design interest)
   Fully adapted > non-adapted:
   • Faster RT (median ~7.2s vs ~9.5s)
   • Higher response rate (96% vs 88%)
   • Semi sits in between on RT/response; accuracy: full 80%, semi 78%, non 71%

   Question-aligned paired non vs full (same question):
   • RT: full clearly faster (all graphs p≈1e-17; 12-node p≈2e-9)
   • Response: full better (p≈.003)
   • Accuracy: full better especially on 12-node (58% → 80%, p=.0001)

4) WHERE DIFFICULTY HITS
   Misses and errors concentrate on 12-node + Q2, especially under non/semi adaptation.
   Full adaptation protects response rate and RT most on those hard cells.

────────────────────────────────────────
TAKEAWAY
────────────────────────────────────────
With N=13 the pilot pattern is clear and consistent:
harder questions and larger graphs cost time, accuracy, and completions;
full visual adaptation helps — strongest evidence on RT and on matched
non vs full comparisons, including accuracy on 12-node graphs.

Caveat: pooled tests treat trials as independent (ignore participant clustering);
still exploratory, but effects are large and directionally stable across people.
